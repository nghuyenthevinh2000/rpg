Transhuman Astartes stand upon their observation bridges with weapons powered down, contemplating a future without endless slaughter.

In the center, Kathryn Vane’s presence radiates the boundless primordial yellow luminescence. The fallen—Bragg, Kroll, Jax, and Malachi—rest peacefully in states of reverent awakening.

The Sanctum of Absolute Light (Former Dread Citadel)The blasphemous obsidian ziggurat of Lord Malgakor has been purified down to its bedrock. A towering monument of yellow translucent crystal rises into the sky, repelling any daemon or warp rift that dares approach the planet.
